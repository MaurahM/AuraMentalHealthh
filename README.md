Aura – AI Mental Health Companion
Overview

Aura is an AI-powered mental health platform designed to provide accessible, culturally aware emotional support, particularly for individuals across Africa where access to mental health services is often limited.

The platform combines AI conversation capabilities with modern web and mobile architecture to create a safe and responsive environment where users can express themselves, receive supportive guidance, and track their emotional well-being.

Aura currently exists as a web application, with a mobile version in development using Kotlin and Android Studio.

Problem

Mental health challenges are rising globally, and particularly in many African communities where:

Access to professional therapists is limited

Mental health services can be expensive

Cultural stigma discourages people from seeking help

There are few digital platforms designed with local cultural context in mind

Aura explores how AI-driven technology can provide immediate, private, and supportive interactions for people who may not have access to traditional therapy resources.

Solution

Aura provides an AI-based conversational companion that allows users to:

Talk about their emotions and experiences

Receive supportive responses and guidance

Access a safe, judgment-free space

Track mental well-being over time

The goal is not to replace therapists, but to provide an accessible first layer of emotional support.

System Architecture

Aura follows a client–server architecture:

The frontend/web client collects user input.

Requests are sent to the Node.js backend via REST APIs.

The backend processes requests and generates responses.

Responses are returned to the user interface.

The mobile application communicates with the same backend services.
